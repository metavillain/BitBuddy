## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)

BitBuddy is a friendly synth for 8-bit "chiptune" music: the sound of old game consoles like the NES,
Game Boy and Commodore 64. Turn your phone sideways and start playing - no music skills needed.

🎹 PLAY RIGHT AWAY
• 31 ready-made sounds: hero leads, bass, chords, drums and sound effects
• SURPRISE ME! for a random sound, MUTATE to nudge it, dice to re-roll one part
• Scale Lock: pick a key and scale and every note fits - no wrong notes
• Magic Chords: hold one key for the classic fast 8-bit chord

🎮 GAME SOUNDS
• Tap COIN, JUMP, LASER, HIT, BOOM, POWER-UP, BLIP or BOMB for a brand new sound effect every time
• Save it as a WAV file for your own game
• Jingles: level clear, game over, got item and fanfare, written in your key

🎤 YOUR VOICE ON THE NES
• Record your voice and play it on the keys, crunched like an old console's sample channel
• HUM: sing a tune and BitBuddy plays it with your sound

🎵 MAKE IT YOURS
• 10 chip sounds, including a drum kit and a wave you draw yourself
• Real NES, Game Boy and C64 modes, a squelchy C64 filter, vibrato, trills, sweeps, sirens
• Game Boy-style stereo, echo, bit crushing
• Play any MIDI file chiptune style: the melody with your sound, triangle bass, drums
• JAM with a little band in your key, and record what you play (WAV + MIDI)
• Share a sound with a friend as a short code

📱 PHONE TRICKS
• Slide a finger up or down on a key to bend the note
• Shake your phone for a new sound, tilt it for wobble

Tap any control to learn what it does - BitBuddy explains everything in plain words.

Free and open source (AGPLv3). No ads, no account, no internet access, no data collected.
