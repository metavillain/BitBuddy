# BitBuddy privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026. Applies to the BitBuddy app (Android, Windows, Mac, iPhone / iPad) and the
BitBuddy plugin.*

**BitBuddy collects no personal data.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. On
Android it doesn't even ask for internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your device

- **The microphone** is only used while you press **REC VOICE** (to record a voice for the VOICE wave) or
  **HUM** (to play the notes you sing). The sound is processed on your device, right away. A REC VOICE
  recording is kept inside BitBuddy's own settings and projects on your device; HUM keeps nothing.
- **The motion sensor** (phones) is only read for SHAKE (a new random sound) and TILT (wobble), on your
  device. Both can be switched off in VIEW.
- **Files you make** (recordings, saved sounds, your presets) are saved on your device, in folders named
  *BitBuddy Recordings* and *BitBuddy Sounds* and in BitBuddy's own app folder.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you do it yourself: when you press SHARE, SAVE WAV or SHOW on a phone, your
phone's own share menu opens and *you* choose where the file or sound code goes.

## Children

BitBuddy collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are published here, with a new date at the top. BitBuddy is open source: you can
read exactly what it does at <https://github.com/metavillain/BitBuddy>. Questions:
[open an issue](https://github.com/metavillain/BitBuddy/issues).
